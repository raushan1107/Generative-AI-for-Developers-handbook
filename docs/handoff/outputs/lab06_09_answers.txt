You have 60 days from the statement date on which the payment first appeared to raise a dispute [1].

[public] My card was stolen and there are payments I didn't make. Will I get the money back, and how fast is a new card?
If you report the card within 2 business days of noticing it's missing, you won't pay for payments you didn't make [1]. If the card is gone for good, a replacement card with a new number will be sent within 5 to 7 business days [2]. You can use a virtual card in your mobile wallet straight away [2].

Sources:
[1] Lost or stolen cards > Your liability (lost-stolen-cards.md)
[2] Lost or stolen cards > Report it and get a replacement (lost-stolen-cards.md)

[staff] Who can approve a fee refund exception of $300?
According to the public fee policy, exceptions from $101 to $500 can be approved by a team lead. Therefore, a team lead can approve a fee refund exception of $300. [2]
