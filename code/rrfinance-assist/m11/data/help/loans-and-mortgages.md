# Loans and mortgages

We offer personal loans, auto loans and mortgages. The rate you are offered depends on your credit history, income and the amount you borrow.

## Personal loans

- Borrow from $2,000 to $50,000, over 12 to 60 months.
- Fixed APR from 7.99% to 19.99%.
- A decision usually within 1 business day; the money arrives the next business day after you sign.
- No fee for paying the loan off early.

## Missed or late payments

Every payment has a 15-day grace period. After that, a late payment fee of $35 applies and the missed payment may be reported to credit reference agencies after 30 days.

## Struggling to pay

If you are worried about a payment, contact us before you miss it. Our hardship programme can offer a payment holiday of up to 3 months, or a lower payment for a while. Interest still builds up during a payment holiday.

## Mortgages

- A mortgage pre-approval is valid for 90 days.
- You need a deposit of at least 5% of the property price.
- You can overpay up to 10% of the balance each year without a fee.

## Credit decisions

We make lending decisions on consistent criteria, never on age, gender, race, religion or other protected characteristics. If we decline an application, you can ask us for the main reasons.
