# Making a complaint

We want to put things right when we get them wrong. You can complain in the app, in chat, by phone or in any branch.

## What happens

- We acknowledge your complaint within 1 business day.
- Most complaints are resolved within 15 business days. If we need longer, we tell you why and when to expect an answer.
- You receive a final response in writing.

## If you are not happy with our answer

If you are not satisfied with our final response, or if 8 weeks have passed without one, you can take your complaint to the independent financial ombudsman or regulator in your country, free of charge. Our final response letter tells you how.

## Putting it right

If we made a mistake, we refund any fees or interest it caused and may offer a goodwill payment. We also tell you what we are changing so it doesn't happen again.

## Help with your complaint

You can ask someone you trust to complain for you, with your permission. If you need the information in large print, braille or another language, tell us.
