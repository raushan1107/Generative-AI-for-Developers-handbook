# Refunds and cancellations

TrainCrest refunds depend on how far ahead of the course start date you cancel. The start date is the first day of the live class or, for self-paced courses, the day your access begins.

## How much you get back

- Cancel 14 or more days before the start date: 100% refund of the course price.
- Cancel 7 to 13 days before the start date: 50% refund.
- Cancel less than 7 days before the start date: no refund. Instead, you can move to a later date once, free of charge.

Fees paid to third parties, such as exam vouchers already issued, are not refundable once the voucher code has been sent to you.

## How to cancel

Open My Learning, choose the enrolment and select Cancel enrolment. You will see the refund amount before you confirm. You can also ask Traincrest Assist to raise a cancellation ticket for you.

## When the money arrives

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. Card refunds can take one extra billing cycle to appear on your statement.

## If TrainCrest cancels

If we cancel a class, for example because the trainer is ill, you choose between a full refund and a free move to another date. Any Retake Pass bought for that course is refunded in full.
