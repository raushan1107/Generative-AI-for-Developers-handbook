# Fees and charges

This article lists our most common personal banking fees. Your account terms list every fee.

## Monthly account fees

- Everyday Checking: $8 a month, waived in any month with a $1,500 daily balance or $500 in direct deposits.
- Student Checking and High-Yield Savings: no monthly fee.
- Premier Checking: $25 a month, waived with $25,000 in combined balances.

## Overdrafts

If a payment takes your balance below zero, we charge an overdraft fee of $29, at most 3 times per day. There is no fee if you are overdrawn by $50 or less at the end of the day. Link a savings account for free automatic overdraft transfers.

## Card and cash fees

- Using another bank's ATM: $3 per withdrawal, plus any fee the ATM owner charges.
- Foreign transaction fee: 3% of each payment in another currency. The Voyager Travel Card has no foreign transaction fee.
- Replacement card express delivery: $15.

## Other fees

- Stop payment on a cheque: $30.
- Paper statements: $2 a month. Online statements are free.

## Fee refunds

We can refund one overdraft fee per account every 12 months as a courtesy. Ask in chat or call us; other fee refunds are reviewed case by case.
