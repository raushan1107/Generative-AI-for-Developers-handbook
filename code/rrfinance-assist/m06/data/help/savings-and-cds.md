# Savings accounts and CDs

Our savings products help you set money aside and earn interest.

## High-Yield Savings

- Variable rate, currently 4.10% APY. We tell you at least 30 days before we lower it.
- No monthly fee and no minimum balance.
- Six free withdrawals or transfers out each month; after that, $5 each.
- Interest is calculated daily and paid monthly.

## Certificates of deposit (CDs)

A CD pays a fixed rate for a fixed term, from a $1,000 minimum deposit.

- 12-Month CD: 4.50% APY. An early withdrawal costs 90 days of interest.
- 24-Month CD: 4.25% APY. An early withdrawal costs 180 days of interest.

You can't add money to a CD after you open it.

## When a CD matures

At maturity you have a 10-day grace period to withdraw the money or change the term without a penalty. If you do nothing, the CD renews automatically for the same term at the rate we offer that day.

## Deposit protection

Deposits are protected by the national deposit insurance scheme up to its limit per customer. Your account terms show the scheme and the current limit.
