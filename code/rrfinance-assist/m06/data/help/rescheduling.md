# Rescheduling a course

Plans change. You can move your enrolment to another date of the same course.

## Fees

- Up to 7 days before the start date: rescheduling is free, as many times as you need.
- Within 7 days of the start date: you can reschedule once for a $50 fee. If you cancelled late and chose the free move offered instead of a refund, that move counts as this one reschedule.
- After the course has started: rescheduling is not possible. Contact support if you missed the class because of an emergency.

## How to reschedule

In My Learning, open the enrolment, choose Change date and pick a new class. Seats are limited, so the dates you see are the ones with space. Your exam voucher and lab access move with you.

## Changing to a different course

Switching to a different course is treated as a cancellation and a new enrolment. The refund rules in the Refunds and cancellations article apply to the original course.
