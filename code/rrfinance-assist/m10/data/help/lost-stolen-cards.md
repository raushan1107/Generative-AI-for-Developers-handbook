# Lost or stolen cards

If your card is lost or stolen, act straight away. The sooner you tell us, the better we can protect your money.

## Freeze it first

In the app, open Cards and choose Freeze card. A freeze takes effect instantly and you can unfreeze the card if you find it. While a card is frozen, payments, ATM withdrawals and contactless payments are declined, but direct debits and subscriptions keep working.

## Report it and get a replacement

If the card is gone for good, choose Report lost or stolen. We cancel the card permanently and send a replacement with a new number within 5 to 7 business days. Express delivery within 2 business days costs $15, and is free for Premier Checking customers. You can use a virtual card in your mobile wallet straight away.

## Your liability

You pay nothing for payments you didn't make if you report the card within 2 business days of noticing it is missing. Remember to update your new card number with any merchants that charge you regularly.

## Card stuck in an ATM

If an ATM keeps your card, report it as lost in the app. For security reasons the card is destroyed and cannot be returned.
