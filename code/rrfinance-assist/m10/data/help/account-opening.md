# Opening an account

You can open most personal accounts in the app in about 10 minutes.

## Who can open an account

- You must be 18 or older, or 17 or older for Student Checking.
- Children under 17 can have a Kids Saver account opened by a parent or guardian.
- You need a residential address in a country where we operate.

## What you need

- A valid government photo ID, such as a passport or national identity card.
- Proof of address dated within the last 90 days, such as a utility bill or a bank statement.
- Your tax identification number.

The law requires us to verify the identity of every customer before we open an account (often called "know your customer", or KYC). We can't open an account without it, and we can't make exceptions.

## How long it takes

Most applications are approved instantly. If we need to check your documents by hand, we decide within 2 business days. If we can't verify your identity, we will ask for more documents rather than decline straight away.

## Business accounts

Business accounts also need the company's registration documents and ID for every owner with 25% or more of the business. Allow up to 5 business days.
