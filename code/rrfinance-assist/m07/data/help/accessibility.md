# Accessibility and learning support

We want every learner to take part fully.

## During live classes

Live online classes include automatic captions. Recordings are available for 30 days after each session for learners who need to review at their own pace.

## Adjustments

Tell us about any adjustments you need, such as extra time on quizzes, screen-reader friendly materials or a sign-language interpreter, at least 10 business days before the course. There is no charge.

## Materials

Course slides and lab guides are available as accessible PDFs and HTML. Videos have captions and transcripts.

## Contact

Write to support and mention "accessibility" in the subject, or ask Traincrest Assist to raise a ticket with the details. Your information is shared only with the people who arrange the support.
