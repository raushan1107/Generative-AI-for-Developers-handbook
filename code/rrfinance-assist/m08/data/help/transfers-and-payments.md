# Transfers and payments

Move money between your own accounts, to other people, or to other banks at home and abroad.

## Timing

- Between your RR Finance Bank accounts: instant, any time.
- Instant Pay to another person: usually within seconds, up to $2,500 a day.
- Standard bank transfers: 1 to 3 business days.
- Domestic wires: same business day if sent before the 4 pm cut-off; otherwise the next business day.
- International wires: 1 to 5 business days, depending on the country and any intermediary banks.

## Limits and fees

Online and app transfers have a daily limit of $10,000. Outgoing domestic wires cost $25 and international wires cost $45; incoming wires cost $15. Premier Checking customers pay no wire fees.

## A payment is pending

A pending payment has left your available balance but is not yet final. Pending transfers usually settle within 3 business days. If a transfer is still pending after 5 business days, ask us to trace it.

## Sent money to the wrong person

Once a transfer or wire is processed, we can't cancel it. We can ask the receiving bank to return the money (a recall request, $25 for wires), but they need the recipient's consent, so the money may not come back. Always check the name and account number before you send.
