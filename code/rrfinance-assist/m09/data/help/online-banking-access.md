# Online banking and app access

Online banking and the RR Finance Bank app let you see your accounts, move money and manage your cards.

## Signing in

Sign in with your customer ID or email and your password, then confirm with a one-time code sent to your phone or a prompt in the app. You can turn on fingerprint or face sign-in in the app settings.

## Forgotten password

Choose Forgot password on the sign-in page. We send a reset link to your registered email; it is valid for 30 minutes. If it doesn't arrive, check your spam folder, then ask us to resend it.

## Locked out

After 5 wrong passwords in a row, your online access is locked for your security. Your cards and accounts keep working. To unlock, reset your password, or call us and pass the security questions.

## New phone

If you change your phone or number, update it in the app before you switch, or call us to re-register your device. For your safety, transfers to new payees are limited to $1,000 for 24 hours after a device change.

## Statements and documents

Statements are available online for 7 years. You can download them as PDF from the Documents section, and request a stamped letter from a branch if you need one for a visa or a mortgage.
