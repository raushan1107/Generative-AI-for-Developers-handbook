# Fraud and scams

If you think someone has accessed your account or used your card, contact us straight away. The fraud team is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

## We will never ask

RR Finance Bank will never ask you for your full PIN, your online banking password or a one-time code, by phone, email, text or chat. We will never ask you to move money to a "safe account". Anyone who does is a fraudster: hang up and call the number on the back of your card.

## A payment you don't recognise

If you don't recognise a payment, freeze your card in the app and report it. We investigate unauthorised payments and refund them, usually within 10 business days. You are not liable for payments you didn't make, unless you shared your security details or acted with gross negligence.

## Scams where you sent the money

If you were tricked into sending a payment yourself (for example a fake investment, a romance scam or a fake invoice), report it within 24 hours. We will try to recover the money from the receiving bank, but recovery is not guaranteed because you authorised the payment.

## While we investigate

We may temporarily block your card or online banking to protect you. We will tell you what is blocked and how to keep paying essential bills.

## Phishing messages

Forward suspicious emails to the fraud team and delete them. Don't click links or open attachments.
