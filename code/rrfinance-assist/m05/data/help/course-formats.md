# Course formats

TrainCrest offers three formats. The content is the same; the way you learn is different.

## Live online

A trainer teaches in real time over video, usually from 9:00 to 17:00 in the class time zone, with breaks. You can ask questions, work in breakout rooms and get help in the labs.

## Classroom

In-person classes at TrainCrest training centres or partner venues. Lunch and refreshments are included. Bring a laptop; we provide lab access.

## Self-paced

Video lessons, readings and labs you complete on your own schedule within 90 days of starting. A forum and weekly office hours with a trainer are included.

## Which one is right for me?

Choose live online or classroom if you like structure and asking questions as you go. Choose self-paced if your schedule is unpredictable. Certification courses are available in all three formats unless the course page says otherwise.
