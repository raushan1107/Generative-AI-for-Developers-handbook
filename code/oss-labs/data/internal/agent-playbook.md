# Contact centre agent playbook

Internal. For RR Finance Bank contact-centre staff only. Customers must not see this page.

## Response targets

- High urgency (fraud, a lost or stolen card, a customer who can't access their money): first response within 15 minutes, 24 hours a day.
- Disputes and fee questions: first response within 1 business day.
- Complaints: acknowledge within 1 business day; the complaints team owns the case after that.

## Security checks

Before discussing an account, confirm the customer's identity with two security questions in the app or on the phone. Never ask for a full card number, a PIN, a password or a one-time code, even when a customer offers it. If a customer reads out a one-time code, stop and warn them that the bank never needs it.

## Goodwill credits

Any agent can give a goodwill credit of up to $25 per customer per year, without approval, when the bank made a mistake. Record the reason in the case.

## When to escalate

Escalate to your team lead when a customer mentions legal action, a regulator or the press, or asks for a fee refund exception over $100. Signs of a vulnerable customer (bereavement, serious illness, financial hardship, signs of coercion) go to the Customer Care team the same day. Requests to access or delete personal data go to the Privacy queue within 24 hours.
