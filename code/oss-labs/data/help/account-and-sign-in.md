# Account and sign-in

## Resetting your password

Select Forgot password on the sign-in page and enter your email address. The reset link is valid for 30 minutes. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive.

## Two-step verification

We recommend turning on two-step verification in Profile, Security. If you lose your phone, contact support from the email address on your account to reset it.

## Changing your email address

Update your email in Profile. We send a confirmation link to the new address, and your sign-in changes only after you confirm it.

## Company accounts

If your employer bought your training, you may sign in with your company account (single sign-on). Ask your company's training coordinator if you can't access it.

## Deleting your account

You can ask us to delete your account and personal data. Certificates already issued remain verifiable unless you ask us to remove them too.
