# Disputing a card transaction

You can dispute a card payment that you recognise but that is wrong: you were charged twice, charged the wrong amount, or paid for goods or services that never arrived. If you don't recognise a payment at all, that is fraud, not a dispute: freeze your card and follow the Fraud and scams article instead.

## Deadlines

- Raise a dispute within 60 days of the statement date on which the payment first appeared.
- After 60 days we can still review it, but card network rules may stop us from getting your money back.

## What happens next

We may ask you to contact the merchant first and keep their reply. Once you raise the dispute, we give you a provisional credit within 10 business days while we investigate. Most investigations finish within 45 days; payments made abroad or on accounts open less than 30 days can take up to 90 days. If the merchant proves the payment was correct, the provisional credit is reversed and we tell you in writing.

## What can't be disputed

A purchase you authorised and later regretted, a price you agreed to, or a subscription you forgot to cancel is not a dispute. Cancel the subscription with the merchant and ask them for a refund.

## How to raise one

In the app, open the transaction and choose Dispute this payment, or ask RR Finance Assist in chat to raise a dispute case for you.
