# Payments and invoices

## Ways to pay

We accept major credit and debit cards, PayPal and bank transfer. Bank transfers must arrive at least 5 business days before the start date to confirm your seat.

## Invoices and receipts

An invoice is emailed when you pay and is always available in My Learning, Billing. If you need a company name, address or tax number on the invoice, add it before you pay or ask support to reissue the invoice.

## Prices and currency

Prices are shown in US dollars. Your bank may convert the amount to your local currency and charge a conversion fee.

## Paying in instalments

Courses over $1,000 can be paid in three monthly instalments with no extra cost. If an instalment fails, we retry after 3 days and pause course access after 10 days.

## Retake Pass

The Retake Pass lets you repeat a live course once for free if you need to. It costs 15% of the course price and must be bought before the course ends. If the course is cancelled, the Retake Pass is refunded in full.
