Fee refund exceptions and approvals

The public fee policy covers most cases. Sometimes we refund a fee as an exception, and this page says who can approve it.

Who can approve what

  • Fee refund exceptions up to $100: any contact-centre agent. Record the reason in the case.
  • Exceptions from $101 to $500: a team lead.
  • Exceptions over $500: the contact-centre manager.

Hardship and bereavement

When a customer is in financial hardship or has been bereaved, refund overdraft and late-payment fees from the last 90 days without counting them against the courtesy refund. Do not attach medical or death certificates to the case: record only who saw the document and when.

When the bank made a mistake

Refund every fee caused by the mistake, as the public policy says, and add a $25 goodwill credit, which needs no approval.
