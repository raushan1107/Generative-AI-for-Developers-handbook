# Support team playbook

Internal. For TrainCrest support staff only. Learners must not see this page.

## Response targets

- High urgency (class starts within 48 hours, lab down during a class): first response within 4 business hours.
- All other tickets: first response within 1 business day.
- Billing and refund tickets: resolved within 3 business days.

## Tone

Use the learner's name. Explain the policy before the exception, never blame the learner, and end every reply with the next step and who owns it.

## Goodwill credits

Any support agent can give a goodwill credit of up to $50 towards a future course, without approval, once per learner per year. Record the reason in the ticket.

## When to escalate

Escalate to your team lead when a learner mentions legal action, complains about a trainer's conduct, or asks for a refund exception over $100. Requests to access or delete personal data go to the Privacy queue in the ticket system within 24 hours.
