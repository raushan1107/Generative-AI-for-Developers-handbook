Refund exceptions and approvals

The public refund policy covers most cases. Sometimes we make an exception, and this page says who can approve it.

Who can approve what

  • Exceptions up to $100: any support agent. Record the reason in the ticket.
  • Exceptions from $101 to $500: a team lead.
  • Exceptions over $500: the support manager.

Medical and bereavement exceptions

With a doctor's note or a similar document, a learner can have a full refund or a free move to another date at any time, even after the course has started. Do not attach the document to the ticket: record only who saw it and when.

When TrainCrest cancels a class

The learner chooses a full refund or a free move to another date, as the public policy says. Also add a $50 goodwill credit, which needs no approval.
