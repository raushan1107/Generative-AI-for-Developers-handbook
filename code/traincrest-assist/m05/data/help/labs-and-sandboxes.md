# Labs and cloud sandboxes

Hands-on courses include a lab environment, such as a cloud sandbox or a virtual machine, so you can practise without using your own subscription.

## How long labs stay open

Labs are available for 30 days after the course ends. You can ask for one free 7-day extension. After that, you can buy 14 more days of lab access for $49.

## Common problems

- Error LAB-409, quota exceeded: the sandbox has limits on virtual machine sizes and regions. Use the sizes listed in the lab guide.
- Error LAB-503, lab won't start: wait two minutes and refresh. Sandboxes are created on demand and the first start is slower.
- Error LAB-440, session expired: lab sessions stop after 4 hours of inactivity. Your work is saved; start the lab again.

## Asking for more time

Ask Traincrest Assist or open a support ticket with your enrolment ID. Extension requests are handled by the lab team, usually within one business day.
