# Certificates of completion

Every TrainCrest course ends with a digital certificate you can download or share on professional networks.

## Who receives a certificate

You receive a certificate if you attended at least 80% of the live sessions. For self-paced courses, finish every module and the final quiz.

## When it arrives

Certificates are emailed within 48 hours after the last day of the course. They also appear in My Learning under Certificates.

## Name corrections

Your certificate shows the name on your profile. Name corrections are free within 30 days of issue: update your profile, then ask support to reissue the certificate. After 30 days, a reissue costs $25.

## Verifying a certificate

Each certificate has a verification link. Employers can open it to confirm the course, date and learner name.
