# Training for teams and companies

## Private classes

Companies can book a private class for 8 or more people. We tailor examples to your industry and can deliver online or at your office.

## Group discounts

- 5 to 9 learners on the same public class: 10% off.
- 10 or more learners: 15% off, plus a dedicated training coordinator.

## Training credits

Companies can buy training credits in advance and use them for any course within 12 months. Unused credits are not refundable but can be transferred to colleagues.

## Reports for managers

Training coordinators see attendance, completion and certificate reports for their team in the company dashboard.
