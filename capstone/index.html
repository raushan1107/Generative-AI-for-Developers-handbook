Capstone Project

The capstone is where the training wheels come off. In Part V you get a different business problem and a brief, not instructions. You design, build, evaluate, secure and present a production-ready GenAI application, using everything from the drills and the RR Finance Assist build.

The problem: an investment research copilot

RR Finance Capital· the bank's fictional investment research desk

RR Finance Capital's analysts cover about 150 listed companies. Every quarter each company publishes a results report and a long annual report, and analysts spend days reading them before they can update their notes. The desk wants a research copilot that summarises a company's quarterly report, extracts the key figures (revenue, operating margin, earnings per share, guidance) into validated JSON, answers analysts' questions with citations to the exact page, and compares this quarter with the last. Every answer must say where it came from, must never present an opinion as a recommendation, and must carry the desk's compliance disclaimer. The reports you'll use are fictional.

How the capstone differs from the build

RR Finance Assist (the build)

  • One milestone per module, with guidance
  • Reference code for every step
  • Same problem all course long: a bank's customer support

The capstone

  • A new business problem: investment research
  • A brief and acceptance criteria, not instructions
  • You choose the architecture, prompts, models and tests, and defend them

What you'll deliver

  1. A design note: patterns used (generate, extract, ground, act…), model choices with evidence, and what is deliberately not GenAI.
  2. A working application: backend service, a simple UI, structured outputs, tools and retrieval where the problem needs them.
  3. Evidence of quality: an evaluation set, measured accuracy and groundedness, and a regression check.
  4. Evidence of safety and cost control: guardrails, least-privilege tools, a cost estimate and a monitoring plan.
  5. A short demo: show it working, show it failing safely, and show the numbers.

How to prepare

  • Finish the RR Finance Assist milestones: they're a rehearsal of everything the capstone asks for.
  • Keep your genai-labs utilities (history trimming, retries, error mapping, output guard, dataset checker): you're allowed to reuse your own code.
  • Practise writing coding-assistant prompts from the Goal / Context / Constraints / Done-when template; you'll lean on your assistant heavily.
