Capstone Project

The capstone is where the training wheels come off. In Part V you get a different business problem and a brief, not instructions. You design, build, evaluate, secure and present a production-ready GenAI application, using everything from the drills and the Traincrest Assist build.

How the capstone differs from the build

Traincrest Assist (the build)

  • One milestone per module, with guidance
  • Reference code for every step
  • Same problem all course long

The capstone

  • A new business problem
  • A brief and acceptance criteria, not instructions
  • You choose the architecture, prompts, models and tests, and defend them

What you'll deliver

  1. A design note: patterns used (generate, extract, ground, act…), model choices with evidence, and what is deliberately not GenAI.
  2. A working application: backend service, a simple UI, structured outputs, tools and retrieval where the problem needs them.
  3. Evidence of quality: an evaluation set, measured accuracy and groundedness, and a regression check.
  4. Evidence of safety and cost control: guardrails, least-privilege tools, a cost estimate and a monitoring plan.
  5. A short demo: show it working, show it failing safely, and show the numbers.

How to prepare

  • Finish the Traincrest Assist milestones: they're a rehearsal of everything the capstone asks for.
  • Keep your genai-labs utilities (history trimming, retries, error mapping, output guard, dataset checker): you're allowed to reuse your own code.
  • Practise writing coding-assistant prompts from the Goal / Context / Constraints / Done-when template; you'll lean on your assistant heavily.
