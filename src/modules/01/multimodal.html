One model, several senses.

Definition

Multimodal models accept and/or produce more than one type of data (modality), such as text, images, audio and video, within a single model, for example answering a text question about an image.

In plain words

Early LLMs only read text. Many current models can also look at a screenshot or a scanned PDF, and some can listen and speak. For you, it means a customer can upload a photo of a statement or a receipt and the assistant can read it, with no separate image system.

WhatOne model that understands text plus images, documents or audio.
WhyUsers send screenshots, photos, scans and voice notes, not tidy text.
WhenError screenshots, receipts and invoices, ID or form extraction, damage photos, voice assistants.

Facts worth remembering

  • Images are converted into tokens too, so a large image costs input tokens like text; apps often resize images first.
  • Not every model is multimodal. Check the model's input modalities in the catalogue before designing around images.
  • Image and speech generation usually need separate, specialised models.
In the real world
In finance

Finance runs on documents that arrive as images: cheques deposited by phone, receipts attached to expense claims and disputes, scanned ID and proof of address for account opening, bank statements submitted with loan applications, and photos of damage for insurance claims. Multimodal models can read all of these in one step. But because a misread digit changes money, banks pair them with checks: totals must add up, a cheque amount in words must match the amount in figures, extracted fields are validated, and low-confidence documents go to a human.

Support tools let customers attach screenshots and let the assistant read them; expense apps read receipt photos; insurers triage damage photos; coding assistants accept a screenshot of a UI bug or a whiteboard sketch. In each case the value is skipping a separate OCR or vision pipeline and reasoning over the image and the text together.

Interview cue

"Multimodal means several input or output types in one model. A customer uploads a photo of their statement, and the same model that chats with them reads the lines and spots the duplicate charge."

Drill 01.12

Read a statement: spot the problem in an image

~12 minAzure OpenAIPython

Goal: send an image and a question in one request, and check whether the model really read it.

Before you start: the lab kit includes data/statement.png, a fictional RR Finance Bank card statement with one problem in it. Look at it yourself first and decide what's wrong. Check that your model lists images as an input modality.

  1. Run 
    
        
    $ python lab01_12_vision.py data/statement.png

    1. Line which looks wrong is "Payment received - thank you" dated 27 Sep. The payment should be
   deducted from the balance, not added to it...
2. It is a payment they made that appears incorrect, but it is not necessarily fraud...
3. The first course of action is to notify the customer that the statement is not accurate...

input tokens (text + image): 1168
    
        
    The right answer: the two SkyHigh Airlines TKT 0172 lines on 9 September for $412.00 are the same ticket charged twice. The customer recognises the payment, so it's a dispute, not fraud, and the agent should raise a dispute for one $412.00 charge (within 60 days of the statement). The 3-billion-parameter model read the text but missed the duplicate, twice in a row, and invented a balance error. Larger vision models (the multimodal models on Azure, Claude, or qwen2.5vl:7b) usually find it.

Open alternative · Claude or a local vision model

      


import base64
from claude_llm import client, CLAUDE_MODEL, FALLBACK, text_of

image_b64 = base64.b64encode(open("data/statement.png", "rb").read()).decode()
msg = client.beta.messages.create(
    model=CLAUDE_MODEL, max_tokens=4000,
    messages=[{"role": "user", "content": [
        {"type": "image", "source": {"type": "base64", "media_type": "image/png", "data": image_b64}},
        {"type": "text", "text": "Which line on this card statement looks wrong, and is it a dispute or fraud? Be brief."},
    ]}],
    **FALLBACK,
)
print(text_of(msg))

Takeaway
Multimodal models remove whole pipelines (OCR, image classifiers), but images are paid for in tokens like everything else.
