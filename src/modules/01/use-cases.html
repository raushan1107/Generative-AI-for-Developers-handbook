Imagine this

The patterns are kitchen techniques: boil, fry, grill, bake. Thousands of dishes, a handful of techniques. "Support bot", "HR helpdesk" and "legal Q&A" look different on the menu, but in the kitchen they're the same dish: ground the model in documents, let it call a few tools.

In the real world
In finance

The same six patterns cover most of what banks and investment firms build. Generate: first drafts of client letters and market commentary. Transform: summarising a complaint file or a 10-K for a credit analyst. Extract: pulling amounts, dates and counterparties from trade confirmations, invoices and loan applications. Search: finding similar past fraud cases or research notes by meaning. Ground: answering staff questions from the policy manual with a citation. Act: freezing a card or opening a dispute through a bank API, with a human approval step. And the not-GenAI list is long in finance: interest, amortisation, fees, risk limits and anything that posts to a ledger stay in tested code.

  • Coding assistants (GitHub Copilot, Claude Code, Codex): generate and transform code, with your files as context, and increasingly act by running commands and tests.
  • Office copilots: summarise meetings and email threads (transform), draft documents (generate), answer from your files (ground).
  • Support assistants in help-desk products: answer from knowledge articles (ground), look up orders or accounts (act), hand over to a human with a summary (transform).
  • Document AI in finance and insurance: extract fields from invoices, claims and contracts into structured data (extract), often from scans (multimodal).
Traditional developer view

"The business wants 'an AI chatbot'. Let's pick a framework."

GenAI developer view

"Which patterns are inside that request? Generate, transform, extract, search, ground, act. Each brings its own architecture, risks and tests."

Takeaway

Translate every GenAI idea into patterns: generate, transform, extract, search, ground, act, and spot the parts that are not GenAI at all.
