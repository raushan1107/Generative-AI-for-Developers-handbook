In the real world

Microsoft Foundry describes evaluation across model selection, application development and production monitoring, including RAG quality, safety and agent task metrics. Evaluators are measurements, not universal correctness certificates. See Microsoft Learn: generative AI observability and evaluation.

In finance

RR Finance Assist tracks dispute-versus-fraud triage separately: a customer who recognizes a merchant but disputes the amount is not the same as a customer who does not recognize the payment. Overall accuracy can hide missed fraud, so fraud recall needs its own threshold and reviewed examples.
