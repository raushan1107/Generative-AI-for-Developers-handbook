Evaluation review.

1. Why is accuracy insufficient for rare fraud reports?

A model can label most ordinary messages correctly while missing a large share of fraud. Report recall and inspect representative fraud cases.

2. What is a model-based evaluator?

A model that scores another output against a rubric or reference. Calibrate it against human judgments and inspect disagreements; it is not an oracle.

3. What belongs in an evaluation record?

At minimum, the dataset version, model/deployment, prompt and tool versions, metric definitions, thresholds, results and review decisions.

4. What is next?

Module 10 treats prompts, tools and customer data as security and responsible-AI concerns, building on the risk measures defined here.

Remember

A metric is evidence about a defined test, not a promise about every future interaction. Keep the test representative and the release decision explicit.
