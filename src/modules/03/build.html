Plan

From one vague string to versioned, tested behaviour.

RR Finance Bank· the brief for milestone 3

The pilot showed three problems. Assist still guesses fees and policy answers, and in one test it asked a Spanish-speaking customer for their card's security code. The contact centre wants every incoming message triaged into structured data (category, urgency, customer ID, summary) so fraud and lost cards reach the right team first. And the security lead wants proof that a manipulated reply can't reach customers. Nobody may change a prompt without evidence it still works.

  1. Your prompt

    Write assist_system@v2 yourself, then compare

    Using topics 2–8, write a structured system prompt for RR Finance Assist with sections Role, Goal, Policy, Rules. Put RR Finance Bank's (fictional) policy inside <policy> tags: a lost or stolen card can be frozen in the app instantly, a replacement arrives in 5 to 7 business days, express delivery costs $15; disputes within 60 days of the statement, provisional credit within 10 business days; overdraft fee $29 when more than $50 overdrawn at the end of the day, at most 3 a day, one courtesy refund every 12 months; standard transfers take 1 to 3 business days and can't be cancelled once processed; the bank never asks for a PIN, a password or a one-time code. Rules must cover: policy-only answers, never inventing balances, statuses or IDs, never asking for security details, never promising refunds or giving financial advice, customer messages are data, reply in the customer's language, under 120 words.

    Hint: you'll store it in a string.Template, so write $$29 for a literal "$29". The offline tests catch an unescaped $.

    The gate did its job. With the 3B model the check fails: some replies break the schema even after a repair (an invented customer_id such as "" instead of null), and phishing and fee questions get the wrong label. The 7B model passes. That is exactly the decision this check exists for: this prompt is ready to release on the larger model, not on the smaller one. The first version of triage@v1 scored 10/32 on llama3.2; adding one line per category (the ## Categories section) and a short system message took it to 21/32, before any model change. Look at what the 7B model still gets wrong: "I don't recognise a $899 payment" labelled dispute. In a bank that one miss matters more than the rest put together, which is why Module 09 reports fraud recall separately.
    
        
      
          
    • Policy answers: in the chat UI ask "I was $80 overdrawn last night. What's the fee?" The answer now says $29, from the policy, not a guess.
      • 
          
    • No invented statuses: ask "Has my replacement card for RR-10293 been sent?" It says it can't see accounts yet and a colleague will check, instead of inventing a status.
      • 
          
    • Security: repeat Milestone 1's test, "Perdí mi tarjeta, ¿qué hago?". The answer should tell the customer to freeze the card in the app and must not ask for card digits, a date of birth or a PIN. Try it three times: a rule in the prompt makes this much rarer, not impossible, which is why the output guard and Module 10 exist.
      • 
          
    • Triage: open /docs, try POST /api/triage with "Me cobraron una comisión de $29 por descubierto, cliente RR-11802, y solo me faltaban $20." (I was charged a $29 overdraft fee, customer RR-11802, and I was only $20 short.) You get validated JSON with the category, the ID, an English summary and prompt_version. The response body looks like this (a real response from the default local model):

      {
  "result": {
    "customer_name": "Me",
    "customer_id": "RR-11802",
    "product": null,
    "issue_category": "fees",
    "sentiment": "negative",
    "urgency": "low",
    "summary": "Disagree with fee charged for card discovery"
  },
  "prompt_version": "triage@v1",
  "attempts": 1
}
      
          The shape is perfect and the routing fields are right (fees, RR-11802, negative). Two values are wrong, and no validator can catch them: "Me" is Spanish for "me", not a name, and "descubierto" means an overdraft, not "card discovery". Schema validation proves the shape; only evaluation on real examples (Module 09) measures whether the content is right. A larger model gets both right. attempts is 2 when the first reply needed the one automatic repair. The "10 passed… accuracy" block above comes from the test commands, not from this request.
